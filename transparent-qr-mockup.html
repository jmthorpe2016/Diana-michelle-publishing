🎨 Transparent QR Code Options

Removing white squares to show gradient background
Current: White Background
Standard QR Code: Black squares on white background. The white squares block the gradient.
✓ Pros:
• Maximum scanability
• Works with all QR scanners
• Industry standard
Option: Transparent Background
Transparent QR Code: Only black squares visible, gradient shows through where white squares used to be.
✓ Pros:
• Beautiful gradient visible
• Unique, artistic look
• Matches theme perfectly
⚠ Cons:
• May be harder to scan (lighter areas)
• Not all scanners work well
• Need to test thoroughly
